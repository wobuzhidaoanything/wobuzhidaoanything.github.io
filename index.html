    How to use

    • Add furniture: paste or drop a product link, or click + Custom. Drag a card from the inventory into the room, or press Add.
    • Move: drag an item. It snaps flush to walls and sits on tables, shelves and rugs. Hold Shift for free movement.
    • Rotate: drag the blue dot on the ring (15° steps, Shift for 1°), or press R.
    • Room shape: Edit room, then drag the blue corners. Double-click a wall to add a corner. Click a wall to add doors and windows.
    • Walk: switch to Walk view and use WASD, dragging to look around.
