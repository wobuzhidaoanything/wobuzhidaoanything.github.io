    Link reader

    When you run Roomcraft with npm start, links are read on this computer and nothing needs setting up. Only set this if you host your own Cloudflare Worker (see worker/README.md).

    How to use

    • Floors: pick a floor from the tabs at the top of the view (or PgUp/PgDn). Upper floors hide so you can see into the one you're on; All floors shows the whole house.
    • Furniture: drag a model card into the room, or press Add. Drag to move (it snaps to walls and sits on tables and rugs), drag the blue dot to rotate.
    • Edit house (E): furniture fades so walls are easy to grab. Drag a corner to move it, drag a wall to push or pull it (neighbouring walls stretch, or new walls are added to make a recess or bay), double-click a wall to add a corner. Drag doors and windows along a wall or onto another wall. Rooms follow the walls automatically.
    • Tools: V select, W wall, D door, N window, O opening, S stairs. Hold Shift while drawing or dragging to turn off snapping.
    • Walk: click the view to look around with the mouse, WASD to walk, Shift to hurry, and take the stairs between floors. Esc releases the mouse.
    • Designs are saved on this device automatically. Export a design file to share it, or a .glb to open in Blender.
